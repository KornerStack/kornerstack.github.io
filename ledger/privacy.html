1. Who we are

Ledger is a personal finance app for tracking subscriptions, budgets, income and mileage. It is made by Kornerstack ("we", "us"). Kornerstack is responsible for the personal information described in this policy, which applies to the Ledger apps for iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up: your email address and password. We store a hashed version of your password, never the password itself.

Profile information

What you provide during onboarding or in Settings: your name, profile photo (optional), country, preferred currency, time zone, tax filing status and state or province, and the goals you selected when you started.

Financial records you enter

Ledger only knows what you type in or attach. Depending on which features you use, this includes:

  • Subscriptions: service names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, payment history and notes.
  • Budgets: budget names and limits, categories, expenses, savings entries and notes.
  • Income: income sources, earnings entries, fees, client names, tax figures and notes.
  • Mileage: trips you log, including date, distance, purpose, mileage rate and the resulting deduction, plus optional start and end locations and notes. Ledger does not use your device's location: trips are entered manually, and the start and end locations are whatever text you type, such as an address or a place name.
  • Tax estimates: quarterly estimates calculated from the income you record.
  • Attachments: receipt and invoice images you choose to attach, from your camera or photo library.

We do not connect to your bank or card accounts, and we never ask for account numbers or card numbers.

Notification settings

If you turn on notifications, we store your notification preferences and a push token that identifies your device to Apple's or Google's notification service.

Private Vault

If you turn on Private Vault, we store your encryption key in a locked form: once locked with your vault passphrase and once with your recovery key, along with the settings needed to unlock it. Your passphrase and recovery key never leave your device, and we can't unlock the key without one of them. See section 6.

Purchase information

If you buy a paid plan, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details. We receive your plan and its expiry date so the app knows which features to unlock.

Usage and diagnostic information

To keep the app working and understand how it is used, we collect:

  • Screen views: which screens are opened, with a random per-launch session ID, your platform (iOS or Android), app version and account ID.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the error message, technical details about where it happened, your platform, app version and account ID. Before an error report leaves your device, we automatically remove amounts, names, notes, email addresses and other financial fields from the technical details attached to it. The error message itself is sent as it is, and may occasionally include text from the screen where the error happened.
  • Server request logs: when the app connects to our server, the server records each request: the address requested, the time, whether it succeeded and how long it took, your IP address, and basic details of the app and operating system making the request. The address requested can include your account ID and the IDs of the records involved. The contents of your records, such as amounts, names and notes, are not logged.

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: storing your records, syncing them across your devices, calculating totals, budget progress and tax estimates, and converting between currencies.
  • To send notifications you've turned on: subscription renewal reminders, quarterly tax reminders, income logging reminders and weekly summaries.
  • To manage your plan: checking whether you have a paid plan and applying the usage limits of your plan.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure: diagnosing errors, preventing abuse and protecting your account.
  • To improve the app: understanding which features are used, from the screen-view data described above.
  • To communicate with you: responding to your requests and sending essential account messages, such as password resets.

We process your information to provide the service you asked for, with your consent (for example, notifications and photo access, which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working well.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use your financial records to train AI models.

5. What stays on your device

  • Sign-in session: stored in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • Private Vault key: if you turn on Private Vault, the unlocked key is kept in the same secure storage on each device you unlock, and removed when you sign out.
  • Offline copy: a copy of your recent records is cached on your device so the app works without a connection. Changes made offline are kept on your device until they sync. Private Vault does not encrypt this on-device copy.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: if you turn on App Lock, your device checks your biometrics. Ledger never receives or stores biometric data.
  • Camera and photos: Ledger only accesses your camera or photo library when you choose to attach a receipt, invoice or profile photo. Only the image you pick is uploaded.

6. Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your account and records are stored on a server we rent from a hosting provider and run ourselves, with backup copies kept by a separate storage provider (see section 4). Our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. Those providers handle it under their own terms and privacy policies.

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to your records is restricted to your own account by server-side rules, attachments can only be viewed through short-lived access links, and we limit internal access to what's needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach where the law requires.

Private Vault (optional). When you turn it on in Settings → Private Vault, your amounts, names, notes, descriptions, client names, locations and tax figures are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, with a key that only your passphrase or recovery key can unlock. Anyone who reads our servers or backups, including us, sees those fields only in encrypted form. Private Vault does not hide everything:

  • Dates, the category or trip purpose you pick from a fixed list, currencies, mileage distances and rates (and the mileage deduction worked out from them), settings, and how many records you have stay readable to us. Budget category names you type yourself are encrypted.
  • Receipts and invoices are not encrypted yet.
  • Your profile, email address, notification settings and the usage and error data described above are not encrypted.
  • Records you saved before turning it on remain in our backups, unencrypted, for up to 35 days.
  • It protects stored data. It cannot protect you if our servers themselves are compromised and changed to work against the app, or if someone has your unlocked device.

If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, we cannot recover your encrypted data.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and records: kept while your account is active, until you delete them.
  • After you delete your account: your account, profile, records, attachments, and the usage and error data linked to your account are deleted immediately. Copies remain in our backups for up to 35 days, and are then deleted.
  • Server request logs: deleted automatically after 5 days, whether or not you delete your account. Copies in backups are deleted up to 35 days after that.
  • Usage and error data: deleted automatically after 90 days, and straight away if you delete your account first. Copies in backups are deleted up to 35 days after that.

8. Your choices and controls

You can do all of the following yourself, in the app:

  • Access and correct your profile and records at any time.
  • Export everything you have entered and your profile settings as a CSV file, from Settings → Data & Privacy → Export all data. Attachments (receipts and invoices) are not in the file; email us and we'll send you a copy of them.
  • Delete your financial records while keeping your account, from Settings → Data & Privacy.
  • Delete your account and everything in it, from Settings → Data & Privacy.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.
  • Revoke camera or photo access in your device settings.

9. Your privacy rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how we handle it, by emailing privacy@kornerstack.com. We aim to respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires, and may need to verify your identity first. We won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Which of the rights below apply to you depends on where you live and the laws that apply there. This section summarizes rights you may have; it is not a statement that Ledger has been certified or audited against any particular law.

Canada

If you live in Canada, you may have rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) or provincial privacy law, including the right to access and correct your personal information. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

If you live in the EEA or the UK, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable format (the in-app CSV export does this, and we'll send attachments on request). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other US states

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the service.

10. Children

Ledger is not directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
